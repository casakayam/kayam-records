# Kayam Records - Brand Guidelines Summary

## Colors
- **Beige** #e4e2dd — Main backgrounds (55-65%)
- **Noir** #000000 — Text, logo (18-22%)
- **Orange vif** #e63f0a — Accents, titles, CTA (7-15%)
- **Taupe** #bbb3a0 — Secondary backgrounds (5-8%)
- **Vert forêt** #074242 — Nature accents (3-7%)
- **Orange chaud** #e6740a — Hover states
- **Terracotta** #ad410c — Pressed/active states
- **Teal** #0d6969 — Secondary accent, links
- **Teal clair** #0c8686 — Highlights, hover teal

## Typography
- **H1** Anton (Google Fonts) — 28-48pt, UPPERCASE, black or orange
- **H2** Brittany (script) — 14-24pt, lowercase, orange on light bg
- **H3** Akshar Bold (Google Fonts) — 12-14pt, title case
- **Body** Akshar Regular — 10-12pt, line-height 1.5x, dark grey #333333
- **Data/labels** DM Mono — 7-9pt, monospace, grey #666666

## Key Associations
- Orange #e63f0a on beige #e4e2dd — signature combo for titles and CTA
- Black on beige — body text
- White on black — inverted (logo, event headers)
- Teal for nature accents, links, photo overlays
- NEVER orange on forest green

## Graphic Elements
- Enso circle (Japanese brush stroke)
- Paint splashes (orange, magenta, teal)
- Decorative dots in orange
- Grey watercolor textures
- Fluid orange lines
- Organic shapes (drops, circles)

## Layout Principles
- Generous margins (min 20mm)
- Beige #e4e2dd backgrounds — avoid pure white
- Anton titles top, Brittany subtitles as counterpoint
- Full-width photos at bottom or horizontal bands
- Textures/circles in background, never foreground
- Controlled asymmetry — dynamic balance

## Photography
- B&W contrasted or desaturated natural color
- Studio in action, nature, community, performances
- Natural light, warm indoor ambiance
- NO artificial poses, saturated filters, generic tourist photos

## Editorial Tone
- Warm and analytical, never corporate
- Peer-to-peer, direct, inspiring, concrete
